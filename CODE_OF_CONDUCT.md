# Code of Conduct

## Our community

RSMalloc welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participation should be possible without harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.

We work on safety-sensitive software. Direct feedback, careful scrutiny, and strong technical disagreement are expected. Respectful participation does not require agreement, praise, or accepting a contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, designs, measurements, and arguments rather than attacking the person presenting them.
- Support technical claims with reasoning or evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and correct mistakes when new evidence appears.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith. Ask for clarification before assuming someone is acting maliciously.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to end unwanted contact.
- Keep discussion relevant, and respect reasonable moderation decisions.

Questioning correctness, challenging benchmark methodology, requesting changes, and declining a PR are not conduct violations by themselves. Blunt wording or profanity alone is not automatically a violation; targeted abuse, harassment, and intimidation are.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Threats, stalking, intimidation, or sustained targeted harassment.
- Discriminatory abuse or exclusion based on personal identity or background.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sexual content directed at participants.
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information without permission.
- Repeated personal insults, deliberate humiliation, or continuing unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or helping with a report.
- Evading moderation restrictions or repeatedly disrupting project spaces after a clear request to stop.

An ordinary technical mistake, an unsuccessful contribution, or disagreement about implementation is not misconduct. Contributions are evaluated under [the contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md), whether written manually or with AI assistance; the submitting person remains responsible for them.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other official community spaces, and when someone is acting in an official project role. Maintainers may also consider targeted harassment of participants outside those spaces when it directly affects their ability to participate safely in the project.

Any resulting moderation action concerns participation in RSMalloc. This policy does not give maintainers authority over unrelated private activity or third-party spaces. Hosting-platform rules continue to apply independently.

## Reporting a concern

The proposed private reporting contact is **metehanzafer@proton.me**.

Please include, when available:

- What happened and where it occurred.
- Relevant links, messages, dates, or other evidence.
- Any immediate safety concern, ongoing unwanted contact, or retaliation.
- How you would prefer to be contacted about the report.

You do not need to confront the person involved before reporting. Avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Share only information necessary to explain the concern.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with information shared only as needed to assess and address the situation. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. This is a volunteer-maintained project, not an emergency service; response times may vary.

If a report concerns the receiving maintainer, an uninvolved maintainer should handle it when one is available. If no independent project reviewer is available, that limitation must be acknowledged rather than presenting the review as independent. Hosting-platform reporting channels remain available for violations of platform rules.

For suspected allocator vulnerabilities, use the reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, redirect or end a discussion, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should consider severity, context, repeated behavior, and the safety of participants. Serious threats or harassment may justify immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

When practical, affected participants should receive a brief explanation of the action and its scope without exposing private reports or personal information. They may request reconsideration through the reporting contact and provide relevant additional information; this does not guarantee a different outcome or an independent reviewer.

Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy. Moderation should address behavior consistently, not punish good-faith technical criticism or favor contributors because of status or contribution size.
